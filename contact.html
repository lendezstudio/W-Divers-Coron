Frequently Asked

A Few Common Questions

Can beginners and certified divers join the same trip?
Yes. Our guests regularly travel in mixed groups — certified divers alongside first-timers — and our team organizes trips around that mix. Let us know each person’s experience level when you message us so we can plan accordingly.
How do I book a trip?
Message us on WhatsApp, by phone, or by email with your preferred dates, group size, and experience levels. We’ll confirm availability and current rates from there.
How much do trips cost?
Rates depend on trip type, duration, and group size, so we haven’t listed fixed prices here. Message us with your dates and group details and we’ll send current pricing.
Is food included on multi-day trips?
Meals onboard are a recurring highlight in guest feedback for our multi-day trips. Confirm exactly what’s included for your specific trip when you book.
What are your operating hours?
We’re open daily, closing around 10 PM. Message us ahead of your visit to confirm hours and availability for your specific dates.
